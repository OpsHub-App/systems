# OpsHub Migration Manager (OMM)

Migrate to Jira from any ALM or DevOps platform with zero disruption to your teams. Full history, relationships, attachments, and traceability preserved.

---

## What OMM Does

OMM moves your data from one platform to another without downtime, without data loss, and without disrupting the teams that depend on these tools every day. Not just the records. The full history: comments, attachments, links between items, parent-child relationships, and custom field values.

## Key Capabilities

- **Zero-disruption migration.** Teams keep working in the source system during migration. No freeze period required
- **Full history preservation.** Every comment, attachment, status change, and relationship migrates with the record
- **Relationship mapping.** Parent-child hierarchies, epics, links, and dependencies transfer intact
- **Reconciliation.** If records changed during migration, OMM detects and reconciles differences
- **Validation reporting.** Post-migration reports confirm data integrity record by record
