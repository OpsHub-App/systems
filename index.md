# OpsHub Integration and Migration Solutions

Over two decades of connecting the tools that engineering, IT, and product teams depend on. No-code configuration. Bidirectional sync. Zero disruption migrations.

## Products

### [OpsHub Integration Manager (OIM)](oim/)
Bidirectional, real-time sync between 70+ ALM, DevOps, ITSM, and CRM tools. Comments, attachments, hierarchies, and custom fields transfer intact. No coding required.

**Popular connectors:**
- [Jira - ServiceNow Integration](oim/connectors/jira-servicenow)
- [Jira - Salesforce Integration](oim/connectors/jira-salesforce)
- [Jira - Azure DevOps Integration](oim/connectors/jira-azure-devops)

### [OpsHub Migration Manager (OMM)](omm/)
Move between ALM and DevOps platforms with zero disruption to your teams. Full history, relationships, and traceability preserved.

**Common migration paths:**
- [Jira Data Center to Jira Cloud](omm/migrations/jira-dc-to-cloud)

### [OpsHub Migrator for Azure DevOps (OM4ADO)](om4ado/)
Purpose-built for teams adopting Microsoft Azure DevOps from legacy platforms.
