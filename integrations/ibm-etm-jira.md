All data flows bidirectionally in real time. Custom field mappings, conditional rules, and conflict resolution ensure your data stays consistent across both platforms.

## Common Use Cases

- QA teams using IBM ETM while developers work in Jira
- Linking test results to Jira issues for complete quality visibility
- Regulated environments needing connected test and development records
