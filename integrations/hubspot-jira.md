## What Gets Synced

Deals, tickets, contacts, notes, tasks, comments, ownership changes, stages, attachments, and status updates

All data flows bidirectionally in real time. Custom field mappings, conditional rules, and conflict resolution ensure your data stays consistent across both platforms.

- Tracking customer requests from CRM through to development completion
