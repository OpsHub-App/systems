## Overview

Product engineering teams manage parts, BOMs, and change orders in Aras PLM. Software and systems engineering teams track development work in Jira. Without integration, the digital thread breaks between product lifecycle management and software development — change orders don't reach developers, and engineering decisions don't trace back to product requirements.

OpsHub Integration Manager connects Aras and Jira with **bi-directional synchronization** — enabling a federated digital thread across PLM and ALM.

## Why Integrate Aras with Jira?

| Challenge Without Integration | With OpsHub Integration |
|---|---|
| Change orders manually communicated to dev teams | ECOs auto-sync to Jira as tasks or stories |
| No traceability between PLM items and dev work | Automated digital thread across systems |
| Product and software teams work in silos | Shared visibility across PLM and ALM |
| Compliance gaps in change management | Full audit trail across both systems |

## What Gets Synced

**Core Entities:**
- Items, parts, and change orders (Aras) ↔ Issues, stories, tasks (Jira)
- Custom item types and properties
- Lifecycle states and workflow transitions

**Rich Data:**
- Descriptions, comments, and notes
- File attachments
- Custom properties mapped to Jira fields

**Digital Thread:**
- PLM item-to-Jira issue traceability
- Change order linkages
- Cross-system dependency tracking

## Use Cases

**Engineering Change Management**
An engineering change order (ECO) in Aras triggers tasks in Jira for the software team. Implementation status flows back to Aras — the product team sees progress without leaving PLM.

**Digital Thread for Regulated Industries**
Defense and aerospace programs require traceability from product requirements through design, implementation, and testing. OpsHub maintains this chain across Aras and Jira automatically.
