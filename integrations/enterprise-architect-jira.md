### What Gets Synced

EA elements, diagrams, packages, operations, attributes, Jira issues, sub-tasks, versions, worklogs, attachments, history, and relationships

All data flows bidirectionally in real time. Custom field mappings, conditional rules, and conflict resolution ensure your data stays consistent across both platforms.

### Common Use Cases

- Systems engineers modeling in EA while developers track work in Jira
- Linking architecture models to development tasks for traceability
- MBSE teams needing visibility between design models and Jira backlogs
